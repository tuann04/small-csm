# Expresso voice prompts

Each `exNN_<style>.wav` joins 4-7 consecutive read sentences of one speaker and one style from the Expresso dataset
(Meta AI, Nguyen et al. 2023, "EXPRESSO: A Benchmark and Analysis of Discrete Expressive Speech Resynthesis"),
taken from the Hugging Face copy `ylacombe/expresso`. They were trimmed, resampled to 24 kHz and peak-normalized.
The `.txt` next to each wav is its transcript.
